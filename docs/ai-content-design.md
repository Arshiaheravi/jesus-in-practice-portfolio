# AI-assisted scripture content: design and implementation boundary

This preserves the product rationale and identifies what is now implemented. The backend includes a rights-gated complete-edition importer, trusted passage retrieval, optional OpenAI structured drafting, explicit human review, immutable publication/withdrawal, and live catalogue/delivery integration. The shipped baseline remains 24 bundled practices: no real corpus has been approved or imported, no live AI request has been verified, and no full-scripture coverage is claimed. Preview mode remains browser-local; production messaging and content capabilities require separate configuration and verification. See [content pipeline](content-pipeline.md) for exact schemas, commands, limits, and source candidates.

## Intended experience

The long-term goal is varied practical learning drawn from an approved, complete edition of each selected scripture collection. A person should receive a reference, a simple explanation, a realistic everyday example, and one concrete action. The experience should make scripture more approachable while leaving a clear route to read its context.

Bible remains the initial choice. Book of Mormon and both collections require explicit selection. Never silently substitute another collection when the chosen source has no suitable lesson. Keep the edition and collection visible; a reference such as Mosiah 2:17 must not be labelled as Bible content. Doctrine and Covenants would be a separately named, separately approved collection.

AI assists editorial drafting only when enabled and configured. The live service selects from the existing bundled practices and eligible human-reviewed publications; a model response cannot enter the delivery pool on its own. This allows a growing library without treating fluent generated wording as evidence that a reference or interpretation is correct. The editor may also create a manual draft from a trusted passage.

## Obtain and verify a corpus first

Choose the exact editions and confirm rights for the actual territories, commercial use, storage, retrieval, model processing, display, and delivery formats. Record the rights holder, permission or licence text, attribution requirements, restrictions, review date, and any expiry. The official scripture website's availability does not grant a licence to bulk-copy its material: its [Terms of Use](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/learn/legal/terms-of-use/go?lang=eng) place restrictions on reuse and automated access. Obtain authorized source files or a suitable licensed service instead of assuming scraping is permitted. Historical text, modern formatting, study notes, and images can have different rights; see [content sources](content-sources.md).

The implemented manifest records the exact edition, immutable text family, provenance URL, normalization notes, artifact SHA-256, and independently reviewed expected verse counts for every supported chapter. The importer checks exact file bytes and rejects missing, duplicate, unknown, and out-of-range records before one atomic import. Current supported inventories are the 66-book English KJV and the 15-book English Book of Mormon; authorized historical numbering must be mapped and independently verified rather than silently relabelled. Source URLs are citations, not remotely fetched inputs. A partial import is never exposed as a complete edition.

Complete corpus coverage and practical lesson coverage are different. Current reports count expected/imported books, chapters and verses; draft/approved versions; current publications; and distinct verses referenced by publications. They do not prove theological coverage or suitability. An explicit passage-exclusion register and editorial backlog remain future workflow improvements. Genealogies, poetry, law, narrative, and teaching passages need different treatment; do not force an isolated sentence into a universal rule to claim that every verse has a daily action.

## Draft, check, review, publish

1. **Choose a passage and context.** An authorized editor supplies the edition, book, chapter and verse range. The repository retrieves the cited verses and bounded surrounding context from the permitted corpus. Model memory is not the source of truth; the editor remains responsible for checking speaker, audience, and wider context that the bounded window may not capture.
2. **Request structured original content.** Ask for a short contextual summary, `plainMeaning`, `everydayExample`, `action`, `reflection`, and `smsText`. The explanation and example should each stay within two sentences. The example must be clearly hypothetical and recognizable in ordinary life. Require a candid insufficient-context result when the input does not support an appropriate practice.
3. **Validate the source mechanically.** The repository constructs the reference and URL from trusted passage identifiers; the response must echo the exact passage key and satisfy strict structured field limits. AI supplies original explanatory fields, not an authoritative scripture quotation. No mechanical check proves the meaning is correct or detects every unintended quotation; the editor checks wording against the source.
4. **Review the meaning and application.** An editor familiar with the relevant tradition reads the passage and surrounding context. The workflow records approval or rejection, reasons, and explicit checks for reference, context, meaning, safety, rights, and original explanation. All checks must pass before publication. Assigning a database role does not establish theological qualifications; arranging qualified review remains the operator's responsibility. Self-review by an authorized editor is currently permitted, so a mandatory two-person policy is not claimed.
5. **Review audience and care.** Check unfamiliar vocabulary, practical feasibility, and tone with the intended audience. Remove pressure to contact someone unsafe, give beyond essential means, conceal abuse, or treat illness and hardship as spiritual failure. Do not promise healing, wealth, guaranteed relationships, salvation, or divine instructions personalized to the recipient.
6. **Publish an immutable revision.** Store the source/edition version, content revision, review status, reviewer/date, and drafting provenance such as model/prompt version if AI was used. Only approved revisions enter the delivery pool. Support correction, withdrawal, and traceability to the exact version shown or sent.

`Practice` remains the display contract. Private editorial records separately retain the edition checksum, passage key, immutable revision, review events, and manual or AI provenance. AI provenance includes model, prompt version, request ID, input hash and generation time. Rights/review notes are not returned in member catalogue responses. Source permission expiry/revocation, withdrawal, and the global rights switch remove imported content from eligibility, including revalidation before a queued message begins submission. These are original explanations and applications, never a new authoritative translation. See the [operator guide](operator-guide.md) for the review and publication interface.

## Random selection with source preferences and variety

Current delivery selection filters by explicit source, topics, active publication, edition rights and channel permission, then deterministically ranks the full eligible bundled/published pool using the occurrence key. It does not fetch only the first page. A full corpus expands the material available for review; it does not make every passage appropriate for an isolated notification. English is the only implemented language.

The Today button avoids an adjacent repeat within its disclosed loaded set, supports loading more, and respects source/topic changes. Delivery selection currently does **not** enforce a recent-history exclusion window, semantic deduplication of equivalent lessons, equal topic weighting, or equal weighting between collections. Those remain product decisions and future work; a larger eligible collection can be selected more often. Do not promise a different lesson every day.

Empty pools remain empty, with an explanation and a route to adjust preferences. Never fabricate new content, secretly widen the scripture source, or claim endless unique lessons. A delivery occurrence stores its rendered body and selected revision; retries cannot redraw a lesson. The implemented schedule, consent and suppression rules apply; see [backend handoff](backend-handoff.md). Completion records store the practice ID, not the original wording revision, so the reading view may show a newer published version.

## Message design and privacy

Email renders the four-part structure with headings and a passage link. SMS retains the collection/reference and concise meaning/example/action. The implemented Twilio adapter counts the final rendered payload, including sender and unsubscribe text, using encoding/segment rules; the worker freezes a per-message quote at the configured CAD rate and rejects messages above the ten-segment cap. A character limit is not a single-segment guarantee; see [Twilio's documentation](https://www.twilio.com/docs/glossary/what-sms-character-limit). Actual delivery and charges still require authorized provider verification.

Draft content without recipient email addresses, phone numbers, private reflections, or identifying circumstances. Source/topic preferences can be sensitive, so selection should use only the minimum explicit preferences needed. Do not infer beliefs or personal vulnerabilities from behaviour to make messages more persuasive. Private journal text must not be submitted to an AI provider by default. Review retention, training/data-use terms, service location, access controls, and deletion before choosing any provider; keep credentials server-side.

Generation is an editorial action, not a dependency of each reminder. The OpenAI Responses adapter uses one bounded request, strict structured output, a timeout, input/output limits, and no automatic paid retry. It rejects incomplete, refused, mismatched or malformed output and returns an honest insufficient-context result when appropriate. A failed draft does not replace an approved version. The request uses `store:false`, which is not a claim of zero provider retention; review applicable provider terms before activation. See [content pipeline](content-pipeline.md) for configuration and exact limits.

## Evidence and outstanding activation work

- **Implemented and tested with isolated fixtures:** checksum/inventory validation, source/use gates, trusted reference retrieval, malformed AI-output rejection, explicit review/publication, withdrawal, full-pool selection, and queued-version revalidation. Synthetic text is not a real scripture edition and mocked responses are not a live model evaluation. The [verification report](verification.md) records executed suites and limitations.
- **Still required:** authorized artifacts, independently checked real inventories, documented edition/territory/use permissions, and qualified review of representative lessons across both collections. Full import does not establish full editorial coverage.
- **Still required:** an explicit supported model/account configuration, provider data-use review, authorized bounded real AI evaluation, and real email/SMS consent, suppression, payload and failure-path checks. No key, paid request, external delivery, or provider result is fabricated.
- **Deferred policy:** recent-history repetition windows, collection/topic weighting, independent second-review enforcement, and explicit excluded-passage tracking.

The design rationale is informed by [NIST's Generative AI Profile](https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence) as a voluntary reference, not a certification. Production activation also depends on [financial operations](financial-operations.md), [production operations](production-operations.md), and the [provider setup](provider-setup.md).
